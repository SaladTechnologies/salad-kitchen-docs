# Welcome to Salad

Salad pays you for your PC's idle time. While you're away, it runs jobs for SaladCloud customers on your graphics card
(and optionally shares bandwidth or mines as a backup) and credits your account with Salad Balance, which you redeem on
the [Storefront](https://salad.com/store) for PayPal, prepaid cards, gift cards, games and subscriptions.

## What your PC needs

The best-paying work - container jobs - needs a Windows 10 or 11 PC with a dedicated graphics card that's listed on the
[Network Monitor](https://salad.com/earn/demand), 32 GB or more RAM, 70 GB free on the C: drive, virtualization enabled,
and a stable internet connection without a VPN. The full list, including what to do with laptops, AMD cards and multiple
GPUs, is on [Is my machine compatible with Salad?](/faq/compatibility/is-my-machine-compatible-with-salad)

## Five steps to your first reward

<Steps>
<Step>

### Download and install

Get the installer from [salad.com/download](https://salad.com/download) and run it. Salad lives in your system tray.
[Downloading Salad](/guides/getting-started/downloading-salad) covers what to check first.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create your account

Click **Log In** in the app. Your browser opens; enter your email and the one-time code we send you (or sign in with
Google). Add a passkey afterwards to protect your balance -
[How to register and log into Salad](/guides/getting-started/how-to-register-and-log-into-salad).

</Step>
<Step>

### Let the setup wizard prepare your machine

The app walks you through whitelisting Salad in your antivirus, installing WSL (the Windows feature that hosts Salad's
container environment) and choosing which workload types to run. Details:
[Setting up the Salad app](/guides/getting-started/setting-up-the-salad-app).

</Step>
<Step>

### Chop

Choose **Chop Now** and leave the PC alone. The first job is a short compatibility check; after it passes, paid jobs
follow when a customer needs your hardware. Set [Auto Start](/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-auto-start) so Salad runs
whenever you step away - [Getting your first job](/guides/getting-started/getting-your-first-job).

</Step>
<Step>

### Redeem

Open the [Storefront](https://salad.com/store) from your profile icon, pick a reward for your region, and click **Buy
Now**. Codes and claim links land in your [Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes) and your
email - [Redeeming your first reward](/guides/getting-started/redeeming-your-first-reward).

</Step>
</Steps>

## What to expect

- **Earnings follow demand.** The [Network Monitor](/faq/jobs/network-monitor) shows the range your card can earn; where
  you land in it depends on the priority each customer sets. Jobs come and go, and "Finding Job" between them is normal.
- **Salad wants the PC to itself.** Games, local AI tools and anything else using the GPU pause jobs. Close them and go
  AFK.
- **Balance is credited while a job runs**, and you can watch it in the
  [Earnings Summary](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary). Estimated earnings are a 24-hour projection, not a promise.
- **Antivirus may complain.** Salad uses WSL and, if you enable mining, well-known open-source miners that security
  products flag by mistake. Whitelist it: [Antivirus guides](/troubleshooting/antivirus).
- **Running several PCs?** Log in on each one; earnings combine on one account. One account per person.
