---
title: What region is this reward for?
description:
  How to find the region and currency of a Storefront reward before you redeem it, and what the region codes in reward
  names mean.
---

The Storefront stocks rewards for many regions. The region and currency are stated in the reward's name, image or
description, so check them before you redeem: there are [no refunds](/rewards/help/i-want-a-refund) for a reward bought
for the wrong region.

Region codes you will see in reward names:

- AR - Argentina
- CN - China
- EU - Countries within the EU that use the Euro
- Global - All regions where the title is supported
- LaM/LatAm - Latin America
- NA - North America
- PL - Poland
- RU - Russia
- TR - Turkey
- UK - United Kingdom

A code sold for one region is not guaranteed to work anywhere else, even in neighboring countries. If you cannot find
your region, see [Can I redeem rewards outside the US?](/rewards/help/can-i-redeem-rewards-outside-the-us).

## Cash-like rewards

PayPal, Venmo and prepaid Visa and Mastercard rewards are delivered through our reward partner Tremendous and can only
be claimed in supported countries:

- [Supported PayPal countries](/rewards/help/supported-paypal-countries)
- [Supported Venmo countries](/rewards/help/supported-venmo-countries)
- [Supported Prepaid Visa and Mastercard countries](/rewards/help/supported-visa-mastercard-countries)

If you already redeemed a reward for the wrong region, see
[I bought a reward for the wrong region](/rewards/help/i-bought-a-reward-for-the-wrong-region).
