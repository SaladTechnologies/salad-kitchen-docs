---
title: Supported Venmo countries
description: Countries where Salad's Venmo rewards can be claimed; Venmo is US-only and paid in USD.
---

Venmo rewards are sourced through a third-party provider and are only available in the United States and its
territories. Check the list below before you redeem: a Venmo reward redeemed from an unsupported country may not be
claimable, and [redemptions are final](/rewards/help/i-want-a-refund).

Supported Countries:

- 🇬🇺 Guam
- 🇵🇷 Puerto Rico
- 🇺🇸 United States
- 🇻🇮 US Virgin Islands

## Supported currencies

Venmo rewards are only available in USD (United States Dollar). It is not possible to receive balance in another
currency by redeeming from the Salad Storefront.
