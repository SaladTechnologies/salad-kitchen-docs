  What Salad's prepaid Visa and Mastercard rewards are, how they are delivered, mobile wallets, and where they are
  supported.
---

Prepaid Visa and Mastercard rewards are digital cards independent from your own bank or card services. Each card comes
with its own card number, security code and expiry date, and can be used almost anywhere Visa or Mastercard is accepted.
Prepaid Mastercard balance comes in denominations of USD $5, $10, $50 and $100.

## How the card is delivered

Cards are delivered by our reward partner Tremendous. Shortly after you redeem, an email with a claim link arrives at
the email address on your Salad account; follow the instructions on that link to get your card details. See
[How to redeem prepaid Mastercards on Salad](/rewards/redeeming-your-rewards/how-to-redeem-prepaid-mastercards-on-salad)
for the steps, and
[I redeemed a reward but haven't received it](/rewards/help/i-redeemed-a-reward-but-havent-received-it) if the email has
not arrived.

## Mobile wallets

You can add the digital card to supported mobile wallets. Wallet transfers may be subject to regional restrictions.

- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- Samsung Pay

## Managing your card

Manage your prepaid Mastercard at [My Prepaid Center](https://myprepaidcenter.com/home).

## Where the cards are supported

Check the [supported Prepaid Visa and Mastercard countries](/rewards/help/supported-visa-mastercard-countries) before
you redeem. Mastercard is not supported in the following regions:

- Russia
- Turkey
- Ukraine

## Card declined?

Using the card is handled by the card issuer, not Salad: see
[Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward) for what to check and who to contact. Redemptions are
[final](/rewards/help/i-want-a-refund).
