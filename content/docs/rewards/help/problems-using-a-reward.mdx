---
title: 'Problems using a reward'
description:
  'Who to contact when a reward arrived but does not work: a declined prepaid card, a store that refuses the code, or a
  membership that will not activate.'
---

Salad's job is to get the reward to you. Once it has been delivered, using it is handled by the company that issued it:
our reward partner Tremendous for PayPal, Venmo and prepaid Visa and Mastercard claim pages, and the game or store for
codes. They can see what happened on their side; Salad cannot.

Make sure you are not actually hitting a delivery problem first:

- Reward never arrived: see
  [I redeemed a reward but haven't received it](/rewards/help/i-redeemed-a-reward-but-havent-received-it).
- Code rejected as invalid or already used: see [My reward code is invalid](/rewards/help/my-reward-code-is-invalid) and
  [My reward says it's already been claimed](/rewards/help/my-reward-says-its-already-been-claimed).

## Prepaid card declined, PayPal or Venmo transfer not arriving

Use the **Help** or **Contact** link on the claim page you received from Tremendous, or reply to the reward email. Send
them:

- the reward email or claim link,
- the card details page or the PayPal email you entered,
- what you tried (the merchant, the amount, the date) and the exact error.

Common causes you can rule out yourself: the merchant does not accept prepaid cards or cards from that region, the
transaction is larger than the card balance (many stores cannot split payment), the billing address was not registered
on the card, or the PayPal email was typed incorrectly on the claim page. See the
[Prepaid Visa and Mastercard FAQ](/rewards/help/prepaid-visa-and-mastercard-faq).

## Store or game will not accept the code, membership not activating

Contact the store or game's own support. Our per-reward guides link to it where we have it, for example
[Riot Games](/rewards/redeeming-your-rewards/how-do-i-redeem-my-riot-games-rewards),
[ExitLag](/rewards/redeeming-your-rewards/how-do-i-redeem-my-exitlag-rewards) or
[Razer Gold](/rewards/redeeming-your-rewards/how-to-redeem-razer-gold-on-salad). Send them the code, the account it was
applied to, and screenshots of the error.

## What to send Salad Support

If the vendor says the code or card was never valid, or you want us to check the delivery, contact
[Salad Support](/contact) with:

- the reward name and reward ID from your [Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes),
- screenshots of the error, and of the vendor's reply if you have one.

<Callout type="warn" title="Redemptions are final">

Support can check and resend a delivery and chase the provider about a faulty code, but cannot refund a reward, swap it
for a different one, or change how it is delivered. See [I want a refund](/rewards/help/i-want-a-refund).

</Callout>
