---
title: I want a refund
description: Why Salad rewards cannot be refunded, exchanged or replaced after redemption, and what you can do instead.
---

Salad does not refund, exchange or replace redeemed rewards. The checkout screen states it before you confirm: **Salad
plays for keeps, no refunds.**

Rewards are delivered digitally. Once a code or claim link has been issued it cannot be returned or invalidated, so your
Salad Balance cannot be returned either. This applies in every case, including:

- you chose the wrong reward, denomination or platform,
- the reward is for [the wrong region](/rewards/help/what-region-is-this-reward-for) or currency,
- your PayPal account is closed or limited, or the merchant you want to use does not accept the card,
- you changed your mind after redeeming.

Salad also does not pay out balance directly or convert one reward into another after redemption.

## What you can do

- If the reward **never arrived**, that is a delivery problem and support can help; see
  [I redeemed a reward but haven't received it](/rewards/help/i-redeemed-a-reward-but-havent-received-it).
- If the code is **invalid or already claimed**, support can check it with the provider; see
  [My reward code is invalid](/rewards/help/my-reward-code-is-invalid).
- If the reward arrived but you **cannot use it**, the vendor's support is the right place; see
  [Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward).
- If you bought the wrong region, see
  [I bought a reward for the wrong region](/rewards/help/i-bought-a-reward-for-the-wrong-region).

The only exception is a redemption the provider cancels (for example, out of stock): your Salad Balance comes back
automatically. For anything else, contact [Salad Support](/contact) and we will do what we can short of a refund.

Before your next redemption, check the reward's name, description and region on its Storefront page; that is where the
region and currency are stated.
