---
title: I bought a reward for the wrong region
description: What happens when you redeem a reward that is not valid in your country, and why Salad cannot exchange it.
---

Salad cannot refund or exchange a reward redeemed for the wrong region. Redemptions are final, as stated at checkout
(see [I want a refund](/rewards/help/i-want-a-refund)).

Rewards only work in the region stated in their name or description, and we cannot guarantee anything outside it. For
example:

- Countries outside the official EU list may not be able to use EU keys.
- Countries outside North America may not be able to use NA keys.
- Prepaid Mastercard is not available in some countries, such as Turkey; see the
  [supported Visa and Mastercard countries](/rewards/help/supported-visa-mastercard-countries).

## What you can do

- Try it anyway. Some digital stores accept codes from other regions; many do not.
- Someone else might want it. You can give or trade the code to a Chef in the right region, but be careful: trade only
  with people you trust and watch out for scams.

Next time, check [what region the reward is for](/rewards/help/what-region-is-this-reward-for) before you click Buy Now.
For PayPal, Venmo and prepaid cards, check the supported-countries lists:
[PayPal](/rewards/help/supported-paypal-countries), [Venmo](/rewards/help/supported-venmo-countries),
[Visa and Mastercard](/rewards/help/supported-visa-mastercard-countries).
