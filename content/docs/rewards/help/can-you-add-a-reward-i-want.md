---
title: Can you add a reward I want?
description: How to suggest a new reward for the Salad Storefront, and what to check before asking.
---

First, search the [Storefront](https://salad.com/store). The game, skin or item you want may already be covered by a
gift card for its store (Steam, Amazon, Xbox, Razer Gold and others), or by a PayPal or prepaid card reward you can
spend anywhere.

If it is not, tell [Salad Support](/contact) which reward you would like and for which region. We pass suggestions on to
the team that sources rewards, but we cannot promise or give a date for when a reward will appear. If a reward exists
but is out of stock, see [Reward out of stock](/rewards/help/reward-out-of-stock).
