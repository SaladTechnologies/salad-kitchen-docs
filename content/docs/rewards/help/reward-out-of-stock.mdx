---
title: Reward out of stock
description:
  Why a Storefront reward shows as out of stock, why Salad cannot say when it will be back, and what to do in the
  meantime.
---

Most rewards on the Storefront come from third-party providers. When the provider has no stock, Salad's listing goes out
of stock too, and it comes back as soon as the provider restocks. Salad does not hold its own inventory of codes and
does not get restock dates from providers, so support cannot tell you when a reward will return.

Some rewards are out of stock for a long time. Amazon.in gift cards, for example, have had a long provider-side outage.

## What to do

- **Check back.** Listings return automatically when the provider restocks; there is nothing to request.
- **Choose another reward.** Many gift cards and cash-like rewards (PayPal, prepaid Visa and Mastercard) cover the same
  stores. Check the [region](/rewards/help/what-region-is-this-reward-for) before you redeem.
- **Change your target.** A targeted reward is only a progress bar. Your Salad Balance stays yours whatever you target,
  so you can switch targets at any time without losing anything, and switch back when the reward is restocked.

<Callout type="info" title="Redeemed and then Canceled?">

If a redemption shows **Canceled** in your [Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes), the provider
could not fulfil it and your Salad Balance is returned automatically. Refresh the app or the website if you do not see
it, then contact [Salad Support](/contact) if it still has not come back.

</Callout>
