---
title: 'Can I redeem rewards outside the US?'
description:
  'Yes: the Storefront has rewards for most countries, as long as you pick the version for your region and currency.'
---

Yes. The Storefront carries rewards for most regions and countries. When you choose a reward, pick the version for your
region or currency: it is stated in the reward's name, image or description (see
[What region is this reward for?](/rewards/help/what-region-is-this-reward-for)). Redemptions are final, so a reward for
the wrong region [cannot be exchanged](/rewards/help/i-bought-a-reward-for-the-wrong-region).

For cash-like rewards, check the supported-countries lists before redeeming:

- [Supported PayPal countries](/rewards/help/supported-paypal-countries)
- [Supported Venmo countries](/rewards/help/supported-venmo-countries) (US territories only)
- [Supported Prepaid Visa and Mastercard countries](/rewards/help/supported-visa-mastercard-countries)

If a reward is not available for your region yet, it may appear later: check back, or
[tell us which reward you would like](/rewards/help/can-you-add-a-reward-i-want).
