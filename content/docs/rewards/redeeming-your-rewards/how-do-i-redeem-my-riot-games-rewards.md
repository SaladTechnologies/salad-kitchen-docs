  Redeem a Valorant or League of Legends gift card code from Salad in-game for Valorant Points or Riot Points.

Valorant and League of Legends gift cards are redeemed in-game. Find your code in your
[Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes), then follow the steps for your game. The points can
then be used to buy weapons, skins and emotes from the game's shop.

## League of Legends Gift Card Redemption

1. Start up the League of Legends client, or create an account at
   [signup.leagueoflegends.com](https://signup.leagueoflegends.com/).
2. Once you've logged in to the League of Legends client, click on the "Purchase RP" button near the top right.
3. Select "Prepaid Cards" and enter the code you received from Salad.
4. Click submit.

## Valorant Gift Card Redemption

1. Start up Valorant.
2. In the top right corner of the main menu, you will see two currencies: Valorant Points and Radianite Points. "Click"
   the circle for Valorant Points (the top circle).
3. On the next screen, navigate to the far right tab that says "Prepaid Cards & Codes".
4. Paste or type in the coupon code you received from Salad and click "Submit".

## Experiencing issues?

If the code is rejected or the points do not appear, contact Riot Games directly on their
[official support page for Valorant](https://support-valorant.riotgames.com/hc/en-us) or
[League of Legends](https://support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/hc/en-us). See
[Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward) for what to send them and what to send Salad.
