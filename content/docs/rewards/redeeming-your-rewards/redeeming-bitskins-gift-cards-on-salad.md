---
title: Redeeming BitSkins gift cards on Salad
description: Apply a BitSkins gift card code from Salad to your BitSkins wallet balance.
---

Once you have redeemed a BitSkins gift card on the Storefront, find the code in your
[Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes) and follow these steps:

1. Log in to [BitSkins.com](http://bitskins.com)
2. Visit the [Balance page](http://bitskins.com/wallet)
3. Click on 'Coupon' to begin the redemption process
4. Enter your Gift Card code.
5. Once you enter your code, click on 'Redeem Coupon'

The gift card balance is now on your [BitSkins.com](http://bitskins.com) account and can be spent on the marketplace.

## Experiencing issues?

If BitSkins rejects the code, contact BitSkins support from their website. See
[Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward) for what to send them and what to send Salad.
