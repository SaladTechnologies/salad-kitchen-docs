---
title: How to redeem Hone Premium
description: Apply a Hone Premium code from Salad to your Hone account.
---

Find your Hone Premium code in your [Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes) or in the redemption
email sent to your Salad account's email address, then apply it to your Hone account:

1. If you haven't already, sign up for Hone [here](http://hone.gg/)
2. Go to the [Premium Redemption page](https://hone.gg/app/redeem)
3. Input your code into the text box and click "Redeem Premium"
4. Hone Premium should be automatically applied to your account

## Experiencing issues?

If you need help with Hone or Hone Premium, or the code is rejected,
[join their Discord server for live support](https://discord.com/invite/hone). See
[Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward) for what to send them and what to send Salad.
