---
title: How to redeem CyberGhost VPN subscriptions
description: Activate a CyberGhost VPN subscription key from Salad on your CyberGhost account.
---

You need a free CyberGhost account; follow their
[account creation guide](https://support.cyberghostvpn.com/hc/en-us/articles/5382058040988-How-to-create-a-CyberGhost-Account)
if you do not have one. Find your activation key in your
[Reward Vault](/rewards/help/reward-vault-and-redemption-codes), then:

1. Go to your online [account management](https://my.cyberghostvpn.com/en_US/login)
2. Log in with your credentials and expand the menu by clicking on your profile logo in the upper right corner of the
   screen
3. Select Account Settings
4. Select Use your activation key
5. Enter your activation key in the specified box, and select Use

## Experiencing issues?

If the key is rejected or you want a more detailed guide, see the
[official CyberGhost VPN activation guide](https://support.cyberghostvpn.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017320500-How-do-I-activate-a-prepaid-or-promotion-key-)
and contact CyberGhost support from there. See [Problems using a reward](/rewards/help/problems-using-a-reward) for what
to send them and what to send Salad.
