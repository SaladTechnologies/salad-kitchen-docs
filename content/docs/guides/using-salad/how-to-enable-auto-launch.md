---
title: How to enable or disable auto launch
description: Control whether the Salad app opens automatically when you log into Windows.
---

Auto Launch opens the Salad app when your PC boots. Combined with
[Auto Start](/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-auto-start), it lets Salad begin Chopping on its own whenever you are
away from your PC. It is **enabled** by default; follow these steps to turn it off or back on.

1. Open the Salad app.

   ![Opening Salad](../../../../content/images/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-or-disable-auto-launch-1.png)

2. Click your profile picture in the top left corner.

   ![Clicking your profile picture](../../../../content/images/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-or-disable-auto-launch-2.png)

3. Click **Settings**.

   ![Opening settings](../../../../content/images/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-or-disable-auto-launch-3.png)

4. Near the bottom of the page, find **Auto Launch** and click **Disable Auto Launch** (or **Enable Auto Launch**).

   ![Finding Auto Launch](../../../../content/images/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-or-disable-auto-launch-4.png)
