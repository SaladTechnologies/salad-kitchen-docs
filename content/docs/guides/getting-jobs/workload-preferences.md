---
title: Adult workload preferences
description: What adult-content workloads are, how to opt out of them, and why the toggle is missing in some countries.
---

You decide which kinds of workloads run on your PC. Some SaladCloud customers run jobs that generate images, text or
video of an adult nature, and you can opt in or out of those at any time.

### What kind of adult content may be generated through container workloads?

Some workloads may generate images, text or video of a mature nature.

### Does adult content remain on my PC after the workload has completed?

No. All data related to a container workload, including any media it generated, is wiped from your system as soon as the
job completes. Container workloads are fully [isolated](/guides/your-pc/what-is-wsl) from Windows, so none of the
content is accessible on your machine while it runs either.

### Will I be processing adult video streams if I enable bandwidth sharing?

No. [Bandwidth sharing](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing) serves mainstream streaming services only.

### How do I make sure my computer is not used for workloads with adult content?

Open [Settings](/guides/using-salad/salad-app-settings) in the Salad app, go to **Workload Preferences** and turn the
adult content workload type off. Salad also asks you to opt in or out of workload types during onboarding and after
major updates.

In countries where adult content is prohibited, Salad automatically disables adult-content workloads on every machine in
that country. The toggle is not shown there and cannot be enabled.

### Will my earnings decrease if I opt out of workloads with adult content?

Your earnings depend on demand on the Salad network, your GPU, and how long your machine is available. Salad aims to
offer a diverse enough range of workloads that you can earn well either way. There may be times when you earn a little
less because demand for adult-content workloads is high, but those customers are not the core of Salad's business.
