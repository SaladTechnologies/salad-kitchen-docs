---
title: Downloading Salad
description: Where to download the official Salad installer and what your PC needs before you install it.
---

Salad runs on [Windows 10 or Windows 11](/faq/compatibility/do-you-support-macos-and-linux) PCs. Before you download,
check the [minimum and recommended system requirements](/faq/compatibility/is-my-machine-compatible-with-salad) so you
know what kind of jobs your machine can expect.

Download the installer from one of the two official sources:

- The [Salad website](https://www.salad.com/download)
- The official [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/SaladTechnologies/Salad-Applications/releases)

Run the installer once the download finishes. On a slower connection the download can take a few minutes.

> Want to make sure the file you downloaded is genuine? See
> [How to check if your Salad installer is safe](/guides/your-pc/how-to-check-if-your-salad-installer-is-safe).

If the download or install fails, see
[I can't get Salad to download](/troubleshooting/pc-configuration/i-cant-get-salad-to-download).

Next step: [register and log into the Salad app](/guides/getting-started/how-to-register-and-log-into-salad).
