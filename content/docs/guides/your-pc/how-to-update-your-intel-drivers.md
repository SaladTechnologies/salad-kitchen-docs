---
title: How to update your Intel drivers
description: Use the Intel Driver & Support Assistant to detect your Intel GPU and install its latest graphics driver.
---

Intel's Driver & Support Assistant detects your Intel hardware and installs the right driver for it.

1. Open the [Intel Driver & Support Assistant](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/detect.html) page.
2. Click **Download now** and run the installer, following the on-screen steps.
3. When the assistant opens, let it scan your PC and install any graphics driver update it finds.
4. Restart your PC, then start Salad.

If the assistant does not find your GPU or the driver keeps failing, do a full
[GPU driver reinstall](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers).
