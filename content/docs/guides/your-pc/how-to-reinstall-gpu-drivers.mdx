---
title: How to reinstall your GPU drivers
description:
  Remove your GPU driver completely with DDU and clean-install the latest version to fix GPU detection and
  degraded-state problems.
---

A clean reinstall removes every trace of the current GPU driver and installs a fresh copy. It fixes most problems that a
plain update does not, because an update keeps the old driver's leftover files and settings.

## When to do this

- Salad shows a degraded state with code **-1**, **11** or **19**: see
  [Degraded state error codes](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/error-codes).
- Salad reports **GPU performance low** while nothing else is using the GPU: see
  [GPU performance low](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/gpu-performance-low).
- Salad [does not detect your GPU](/guides/your-pc/what-gpu-or-cpu-is-salad-detecting) even though Windows does, or the
  driver keeps reporting errors or crashing.

Check whether you have an NVIDIA, AMD or Intel GPU first:
[How to find your GPU or CPU specifications](/guides/your-pc/how-to-find-your-gpu-or-cpu).

<Steps>
<Step>

### Download the new driver first

Get the latest driver from the manufacturer before you uninstall anything, so you are not stuck without a working
display driver:

- [NVIDIA drivers](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/)
- [AMD drivers](https://www.amd.com/en/support/download/drivers.html)
- [Intel Driver & Support Assistant](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/support/detect.html)

Stop Salad before you continue.

</Step>
<Step>

### Uninstall the current driver

The most thorough method is Display Driver Uninstaller (DDU), a free tool trusted by the PC community for years:

1. Download DDU from [guru3d.com](https://www.guru3d.com/download/display-driver-uninstaller-download) and extract it.
2. Open DDU. In the drop-down on the right, choose **GPU** and then your GPU brand.
3. Click **Clean and restart**. DDU removes the driver and reboots your PC.

If you would rather not use DDU, uninstall the driver from Windows instead: open **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**,
uninstall the NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics driver package, and restart.

After the reboot your screen may look blurry or oversized and only one monitor may work. That is normal until the new
driver is installed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Clean-install the new driver

Run the installer you downloaded in step 1:

- **NVIDIA:** choose **Custom (Advanced)**, then tick **Perform a clean installation**.
- **AMD:** choose **Factory Reset** when the installer offers it.
- **Intel:** follow the assistant's prompts.

</Step>
<Step>

### Reboot and test

Restart your PC once more, then start Salad and let it run. Check the **Performance** tab to confirm your GPU is
detected.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Still stuck?

If the same degraded state or detection problem comes back after a clean reinstall, run Windows Update, then send
[Salad Support](/contact) your machine ID, a screenshot of the exact notification and your zipped logs. See
[How to create a support ticket](/guides/your-pc/how-to-create-a-support-ticket).
