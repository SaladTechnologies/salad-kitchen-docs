---
title: Does Salad use all of my PC's Resources?
---

Salad may use all of your PCs resources whilst running, depending on the job you're currently working.

## Does Salad use all of my GPU?

For GPU Container Workloads, Salad will fully utilize your Graphics Card (GPU) while running. This means that
applications which rely on your GPU won't be able to be run. This includes gaming, media applications, and local LLMs.
Even though some programs may function perfectly fine while Salad is running, we recommend only using Salad while AFK to
prevent any performance issues.

While running intensive apps alongside Salad, the app may temporarily block workloads from running on your machine to
ensure the health of the network. This includes games, rendering apps, or other intensive processes, and you will have
to close the intensive app before starting to Chop again in order to remove the workload block.

---

## Does Salad use all of my CPU?

Salad does not use your Central Processing Unit (CPU) for mining, but it is utilized for Container workloads. The load
on your CPU will depend on the workload you're running, and it may impact your PC's performance if you're using your PC
while running Salad.

---

## Does Salad use all of my internet?

If you're choosing to earn via Bandwidth Sharing, Chopping Salad may use a significant portion of your available network
bandwidth. If you're having trouble using the internet, we recommend stopping Salad to do so.
