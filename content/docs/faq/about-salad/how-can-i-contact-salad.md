---
title: How can I contact Salad?
description:
  Email support@salad.com for anything account-related, or reach the team and the community on Discord and Reddit.
---

Email [support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com). That reaches the support team, who answer directly or pass your
message to the right people. Use it for anything about your account, balance, rewards or a problem with the app.

For a problem with the app, send your logs with the email so support can look at them straight away - see
[How to create a support ticket](/guides/your-pc/how-to-create-a-support-ticket).

You can also reach the team on the [Salad Discord](https://discord.gg/salad) and on
[Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaladChefs/), where other Chefs can often help as well. All the contact options are
listed on the [Salad Support](/contact) page.

For partnership, affiliate or job enquiries, see
[Partnerships and working with Salad](/faq/about-salad/partnerships-and-working-with-salad).
