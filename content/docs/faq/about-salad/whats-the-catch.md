---
title: Salad sounds too good to be true. What's the catch?
description:
  The real limitations of Salad - electricity cost, job availability and hardware requirements - so you can decide with
  open eyes.
---

There is no hidden catch, but there are limitations worth knowing before you start:

- **Electricity costs money.** Your GPU draws power while it Chops. Container jobs usually pay well above the cost of
  the electricity they use, but mining often pays only a little more than it costs, and on some tariffs it can lose
  money. See
  [Calculating profitability and electricity costs](/faq/jobs/calculating-profitability-and-electricity-costs).
- **Container jobs are not always available.** Jobs come from SaladCloud customers and are assigned to the machines that
  fit them, so there can be hours or days without one. That is why Salad keeps
  [mining as a backup workload](/faq/jobs/mining-as-a-backup-workload): it sets a floor so you earn something whenever
  you Chop. See [How do I get container jobs?](/faq/jobs/how-do-i-get-container-jobs).
- **Earnings depend on your hardware.** Newer GPUs with more VRAM get more and better-paying jobs. An old or low-end
  card may earn very little.
- **Rewards are the only way out.** Salad Balance is spent in the Storefront on gift cards, games, PayPal and similar
  rewards; it cannot be withdrawn to a bank account. Reward prices include a margin for Salad.

When no workload is running, Salad idles and uses very little power, so you are not paying for electricity while earning
nothing.
