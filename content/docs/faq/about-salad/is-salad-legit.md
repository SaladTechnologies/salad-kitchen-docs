---
title: Is Salad legit, or is it a scam?
description:
  Salad is a real company that rents out Chefs' idle GPUs to cloud customers. How to verify that for yourself.
---

Salad is legit. Salad Technologies runs SaladCloud, a distributed cloud that rents the idle GPUs of Chefs like you to
companies running AI inference and other container jobs. Those customers pay Salad; Salad pays you in Salad Balance,
which you redeem in the Storefront. When no customer job is available, your PC falls back to
[mining as a backup workload](/faq/jobs/mining-as-a-backup-workload).

Salad does not sell your data and has nothing to gain from scamming you: the business only works if Chefs keep their PCs
on the network. See [What is Salad's business model?](/faq/about-salad/what-is-salads-business-model) for the details,
and [What's the catch?](/faq/about-salad/whats-the-catch) for the limitations we would rather you knew up front.

## Check for yourself

- Ask in the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/salad) or on [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaladChefs/) - both are
  full of Chefs who have been redeeming rewards for years.
- Read the [Trustpilot reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/salad.com).
- Read the [Privacy Policy](https://salad.com/privacy/) and the [Terms](https://salad.com/terms/chefs/).
- If your antivirus flagged the installer, see [Is Salad a virus?](/faq/about-salad/is-salad-a-virus).
