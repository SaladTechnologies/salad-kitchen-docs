## Partnerships and affiliates

If you create content, run a community or have a business that could work with Salad, apply through the affiliate page
at [salad.com/affiliate](https://salad.com/affiliate). Tell us who you are, what you do and what you have in mind.
Requests sent to support are redirected there.

## Running a fleet of machines

Salad does not give individual Chefs access to the Linux version of the app, and support cannot grant it on request.
Fleet operators and data centres with many machines are a different case: see
[Linux onboarding](/guides/getting-started/linux-onboarding) for who it is for and how to apply.

## Jobs at Salad

Open positions are posted on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/saladtechnologies). Applications go through
there, not through support.

## Everything else

For anything that does not fit the above, email [support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com) or ask on the
[Salad Discord](https://discord.gg/salad), and we will point you to the right person.
