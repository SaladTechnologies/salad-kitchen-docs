## Partnerships and affiliates

If you create content, run a community or have a business that could work with Salad, apply through our support team by
sending an email to <support@salad.com>. Tell us who you are, what you do and what you have in mind.

## Jobs at Salad

Open positions are posted on [our jobs page](https://ats.rippling.com/salad/jobs). Applications go through there, not
through support.

## Everything else

For anything that does not fit the above, email [support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com) or ask on the
[Salad Discord](https://discord.gg/salad), and we will point you to the right person.
