The two places where Chefs actually talk to each other and to the Salad team:

- [Discord](https://discord.gg/salad) - the main community, with help channels and the Salad team present.
- [Reddit (r/SaladChefs)](https://www.reddit.com/r/SaladChefs/)

For news and announcements:

- [X (Twitter)](https://twitter.com/Salad_Chefs)
- [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/saladchefs/)
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/c/SaladChefs)

For anything about your own account, email [Salad Support](/contact) rather than posting account details publicly.
