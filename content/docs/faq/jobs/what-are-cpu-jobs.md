---
title: What are CPU jobs?
description: What CPU jobs are, what your PC needs to get them, and what they earn.
---

CPU jobs are container jobs that run on your processor and leave your GPU alone. Customers use them for lighter, more
general work: collecting regional pricing data, data processing, document parsing, video transcoding and audio
processing.

## Why would I want CPU jobs?

- Your PC has no dedicated GPU, or its GPU isn't one of the
  [compatible GPUs](/faq/compatibility/compatible-gpus-and-earning-rates).
- Your GPU is [in low demand right now](https://salad.com/earn/demand).
- You want to earn while drawing less power. Depending on your hardware, a CPU job can use less electricity than a GPU
  job.

## What does my PC need?

- **CPU:** a 64-bit Intel or AMD processor from roughly the last ten years, with
  [virtualization turned on](/faq/compatibility/does-my-pc-support-virtualization). At least 2 cores and 2 threads. 4
  cores and 8 threads or more is recommended.
- **RAM:** 16 GB or more.
- **Internet:** at least 20 Mbps download. 100 Mbps gets you access to more jobs.

Then [enable your CPU](/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-and-disable-hardware) in the Salad app.

## How much do CPU jobs earn?

Most CPU jobs earn between $0 and $2 a day. The smallest ones (1 core, 1 GB of RAM) earn around $0.11 a day, and about
$1 a day is a fair expectation for a typical PC.

A few pay much more. Around a third of CPU jobs earn $0.06 an hour, which is more than many lower-end GPUs earn. We've
seen some CPU jobs earn over $0.10 an hour, though you shouldn't expect that steadily. Those jobs ask for more cores and
RAM, so the PCs that get them tend to be well equipped all round.

## Will a CPU job use my whole PC?

Often not. Many CPU jobs only need a few cores and a little RAM. See
[Why is Salad not fully using my machine?](/faq/compatibility/why-is-salad-not-fully-using-my-machine)
