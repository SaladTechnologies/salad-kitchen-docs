---
title: Internet and data use
description: How much internet data Salad uses while Chopping, what uses it, and what to do if your plan has a data cap.
---

Salad works best on an internet plan with no data cap. If yours has one, read this before you leave Salad running around
the clock.

## How much data does Salad use?

Chopping 24/7 can use 1 TB of data a month, and sometimes more if your hardware is in demand. Most of that is downloads.

## What uses the data?

Mostly job downloads. Every container job downloads to your PC before it starts. Most are 2 to 5 GB, and some are as
large as 20 to 30 GB. Jobs come and go through the day, so those downloads add up. Once a job is running, it usually
uses very little.

Two smaller things use data too:

- Salad runs a quick speed test each time it starts up or your PC reboots.
- If you have bandwidth sharing turned on, that uses your connection, mostly for uploads.

For more, see [What is bandwidth sharing?](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing)

## Can I run Salad on a plan with a data cap?

Yes, but check your cap first. Chopping 24/7 can use 1 TB a month or more, so compare that to your cap before you Chop
full time. If your cap is lower, Chop fewer hours or turn off bandwidth sharing.

## How do I use less data?

- Chop fewer hours. You can set the hours Salad runs with [Schedule chopping](/guides/using-salad/schedule-chopping).
- Turn off bandwidth sharing.

## How do I check how much data I've used?

Your internet provider's usage meter is the best place to keep an eye on it.

## My plan is unlimited. Is there anything to watch for?

Upload limits. Some plans that are sold as unlimited still limit how much you can upload each month. This is more common
on cable than on fiber, and it matters most if you share bandwidth.
