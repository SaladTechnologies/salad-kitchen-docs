title: Internet and Data Use
description:
  How much internet data Salad can use while Chopping, where it goes, and what to do if your plan has a data cap.
---

Salad works best on an internet plan with no data cap. If yours has one, read this before you leave Salad running around
the clock.

## How Much Data Salad Uses

Chopping 24/7 can use 1 TB of data a month, and sometimes more if your hardware is in demand. Most of that is downloads.

## Where the Data Goes

Every container job downloads to your PC before it starts. Most are 2 to 5 GB, and some are as large as 20 to 30 GB.
Jobs come and go through the day, so those downloads add up. Once a job is running, it usually uses very little.

Salad also runs a quick speed test each time it starts up or your PC reboots.

If you have [bandwidth sharing](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing) turned on, that uses your connection too, mostly
for uploads.

## If Your Plan Has a Data Cap

Check your cap against the numbers above before you Chop full time. You can cut Salad's data use by Chopping fewer hours
with [Schedule Chopping](/guides/using-salad/schedule-chopping) or by turning off bandwidth sharing. Your internet
provider's usage meter is the best place to keep an eye on it.

## Watch for Upload Limits

Some plans that are sold as unlimited still limit how much you can upload each month. This is more common on cable than
on fiber, and it matters most if you share bandwidth.
