  Why your PC runs warmer and louder while Chopping, what is normal, how hot is too hot, and how to keep it cool.
---

Chopping works your PC about as hard as a demanding game, so it runs warmer and louder than it does sitting idle.

## Is it normal for my PC to get hot and loud while Chopping?

Yes. Your fans spin up while a job is running and settle down between jobs. The room your PC is in can get warmer too,
the same as during a long gaming session.

## Does heat lower my earnings?

No. Temperature has no effect on what you earn. The only thing to watch is keeping your GPU at a safe temperature,
because a PC that overheats will likely shut itself down, and that interrupts your job.

## How hot is too hot?

Keep the GPU core under 90 °C. Free tools like HWiNFO show the temperature.

## How do I keep my PC cool?

- Dust your PC every 3 to 6 months.
- Keep it off carpet, with the vents unblocked.

For the full maintenance list, see [Is Salad safe for my PC?](/faq/compatibility/is-salad-safe-for-my-pc)

## Is Chopping on a laptop different?

Laptops are compatible with Salad, but they are more susceptible to heat issues. You may need to take extra steps, like
additional cooling, to keep a laptop Chopping stably.

For more, see [Chopping on a laptop](/faq/jobs/whats-normal-while-chopping#chopping-on-a-laptop).

## What if the noise bothers me?

Set Salad to run during the hours you're out or asleep with [Schedule chopping](/guides/using-salad/schedule-chopping).
If a fan is suddenly much louder than it used to be, it probably needs cleaning or replacing.
