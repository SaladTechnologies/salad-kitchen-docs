---
title: Heat and Fan Noise
description: Why your PC runs warmer and louder while Chopping, what is normal, and how to keep it cool.
---

Chopping works your PC about as hard as a demanding game, so it runs warmer and louder than it does sitting idle. That's
normal.

## What to Expect

Your fans spin up while a job is running and settle down between jobs. The room your PC is in can get warmer too, the
same as during a long gaming session.

## Heat Doesn't Lower Your Earnings

Temperature has no effect on what you earn. The only thing to watch is keeping your GPU at a safe temperature, because a
PC that overheats will likely shut itself down, and that interrupts your job.

## Keep It Cool

- Keep the GPU core under 90 °C. Free tools like HWiNFO show it.
- Dust your PC every 3 to 6 months.
- Keep it off carpet, with the vents unblocked.

[Is Salad safe for my PC?](/faq/compatibility/is-salad-safe-for-my-pc) has the full maintenance list.

## If the Noise Bothers You

[Schedule Chopping](/guides/using-salad/schedule-chopping) for the hours you're out or asleep. If a fan is suddenly much
louder than it used to be, it probably needs cleaning or replacing.
