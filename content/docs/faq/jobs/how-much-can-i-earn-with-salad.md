---
title: How much can I earn with Salad?
description: What decides your Salad earnings, and where to see realistic rates for your hardware.
---

It depends on your hardware, how long you run Salad, and what SaladCloud customers need at the time. There is no fixed
rate. The [Network Monitor](/faq/jobs/network-monitor) shows, for each supported GPU, the lowest and highest rate a job
can pay and the current demand level; that is the realistic range for your card.

## What your earnings depend on

- **Your GPU.** Customers request specific GPU models. In-demand cards get more jobs and higher rates.
- **Job priority.** Each customer sets a priority for their workload, and that priority sets the rate you are paid while
  their job runs. The same GPU earns different amounts on different jobs.
- **Demand.** How much of your GPU model is in use across the network decides how often you get a job and how long you
  wait in **Finding Job** between them. Demand changes hour to hour and day to day.
- **Uptime.** Jobs are assigned to machines that are online and Chopping. The more hours you run, the more jobs you get.
- **Your internet speed and region**, for [bandwidth sharing](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing).
- **Crypto market conditions**, only if you leave [mining](/faq/jobs/mining-as-a-backup-workload) enabled as a backup.

Container jobs and bandwidth sharing depend on customer demand, not on a market, so they are less constant than mining
was. You should not expect a container job to be running every minute of the day; gaps between jobs are normal and are
reflected in the Network Monitor's demand level for your GPU.

To see what you actually earned, use your [earnings history](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary). For ways to improve
your rate, see [How can I earn more with Salad?](/faq/jobs/how-can-i-earn-more-with-salad)
