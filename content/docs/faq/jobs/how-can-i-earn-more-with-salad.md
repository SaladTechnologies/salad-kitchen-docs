---
title: How can I earn more with Salad?
description: The settings and habits that most improve how much Salad Balance your machine earns.
---

Job assignment is automatic, so the biggest lever you have is simple: keep an eligible machine online, stable and
Chopping for as many hours as possible.

- **Run Salad as much as you can**, especially overnight and while you are away. Turn off
  [sleep mode](/guides/using-salad/how-to-disable-sleep-mode) so your PC doesn't power down mid-job, and enable
  [auto-launch](/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-auto-launch) and
  [auto-start](/guides/using-salad/how-to-enable-auto-start) with a short delay so Salad begins Chopping as soon as you
  go idle.
- **Clear your Chopping Power checklist.** The [Chopping Power](/guides/using-salad/chopping-power) bar in the app lists
  what your machine is missing for container jobs: RAM, free space on C:, virtualization, WSL, internet speed. Each item
  you fix makes you eligible for more jobs.
- **Enable your hardware.** Enable your GPU and, if it is a capable one, your CPU too. See
  [Should I enable my GPU, my CPU, or both?](/faq/compatibility/should-i-enable-my-gpu-my-cpu-or-both)
- **Don't power limit or underclock your GPU.** It triggers a "GPU Power Limit Set" notification and blocks container
  jobs. See [How power limiting impacts your earnings](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/gpu-power-limit).
- **Close background programs.** Games, renderers and other GPU-heavy apps pause jobs; see
  [Close games and other programs](/guides/getting-jobs/close-games-and-other-programs).
- **Keep your drivers and Windows updates under control** so a surprise restart doesn't drop a job; see
  [How Windows updates affect Salad](/faq/jobs/how-windows-updates-affect-salad).
- **Check the Network Monitor before upgrading.** If you are buying hardware anyway, the
  [Network Monitor](/faq/jobs/network-monitor) shows which GPUs are in demand and what they pay. Only one GPU per
  machine earns, so a second card does not help; see
  [Can I use multiple GPUs?](/faq/compatibility/can-i-use-multiple-gpus)
- **Join the community.** Our [Discord](http://discord.gg/salad) regularly runs contests with Salad Balance prizes;
  check the events channel.
