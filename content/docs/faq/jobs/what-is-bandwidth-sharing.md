Bandwidth sharing is one of the three workloads Salad can run. Your unused upload and download capacity is used to relay
video content for premium streaming platforms over private, anonymous networks. It needs no special hardware and runs at
the same time as container jobs or mining, so it adds to your earnings rather than replacing them.

Whether you receive bandwidth jobs depends on your region and your internet speed. See
[Bandwidth sharing troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/not-earning/bandwidth-sharing-troubleshooting) for eligible regions
and [Getting bandwidth sharing jobs](/guides/getting-jobs/getting-bandwidth-sharing-jobs) for setup. Speed requirements
are in
[What is the minimum recommended internet speed?](/faq/compatibility/what-is-the-minimum-recommended-internet-speed-for-salad)

## Privacy

Traffic is encrypted and anonymous. You can't see what anyone is streaming, and they can't see you.

## The risks

Two things can happen, both uncommon:

1. **ISP data cap.** Bandwidth sharing increases your monthly usage. If your plan has a cap, keep an eye on it to avoid
   throttling or extra charges.
2. **Temporary streaming-service restriction.** Streaming platforms have regional libraries. Occasionally one may limit
   your IP address to its global library for a day or two. Residential IPs rotate often, so these restrictions don't
   last.

If you think a streaming service has restricted your IP: restart your router, or ask your ISP for a new IP address (some
provide this in their customer dashboard). Normal access returns on its own after a short time either way.
