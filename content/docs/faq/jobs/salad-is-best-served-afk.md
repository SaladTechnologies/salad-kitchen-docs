title: Salad Is Best Served AFK
description: How to keep jobs coming to your PC - step away, keep it Chopping, and run your GPU at full power.
---

Your PC earns the most when Salad has it to itself. Here's how to keep the jobs coming.

## Step Away and Let It Chop

Salad is made to run while you're off doing something else. Games and other heavy programs will stop Salad on your
machine until you close them. See [Close games and other programs](/guides/getting-jobs/close-games-and-other-programs)
and the [Intensive Process List](/guides/getting-jobs/intensive-process-list).

If you need the PC back for any reason, just stop Chopping.

## More Chopping Time, More Salad Balance

The longer your machine runs without interruptions, the better its odds of getting higher priority jobs.
[Schedule Chopping](/guides/using-salad/schedule-chopping) for the hours you're away and let it add up.

## Run Your GPU at Full Power

Customers pay for your GPU's full performance, so power limiting is a no go. A power-limited GPU can't get container
jobs until the limit is removed. See [GPU power limit set](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/gpu-power-limit).

## Keep Your PC Maintained

A clean, well-cooled PC handles Chopping fine. See
[Is Salad safe for my PC?](/faq/compatibility/is-salad-safe-for-my-pc).
