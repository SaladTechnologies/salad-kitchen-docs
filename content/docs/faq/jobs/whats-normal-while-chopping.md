title: What's normal while Chopping
description:
  What a healthy machine looks like while Chopping - waits between jobs, changing rates, and when to take a closer look.
---

Salad doesn't look busy every minute, and your earnings won't move in a straight line. Here's what that looks like when
everything is working.

## There is no queue

Salad doesn't line GPUs up and hand out jobs in order. When a customer needs GPUs, we pull from the machines that are
compatible with the job and have been reliable, and after that it's luck. You can never put yourself at the front of the
line, because there isn't one. What you can do is be a world-class Chef:

- Run a lot.
- Don't interrupt jobs all the time.
- Don't use the PC while you're Chopping.
- Have in-demand hardware.
- Have good supporting hardware. More RAM means more chances at better jobs, because some jobs require it and fewer
  machines can take them.

## "Finding Job" shows up a lot

A few minutes is typical, though occasionally it can take a couple hours of "finding a job" before you start earning.
When you start up after taking a break, it may take longer to "find a job" than if you're continually Chopping. Just
leave Salad running, if you can. Restarting doesn't get you a job any sooner, and
[can actually negatively impact your earnings](/guides/getting-jobs/restarting-salad-for-new-jobs).

## Your earning rate jumps around

Every job pays the rate its customer picked, so one job might pay more than your last and the next might pay less.
Between jobs you'll see the rate sit at zero for a bit. That's the gap before your next job, and it goes back up once
one starts. See [Why is my earning rate sporadic?](/faq/jobs/why-is-my-earning-rate-sporadic-random).

## Estimated earnings keep changing

That number is a [24-hour projection](/faq/jobs/how-estimated-earnings-work) based on the job you're running right now,
so it moves whenever your job does. If you want to see what you've really earned, check your
[Earnings Summary](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary).

## Your balance takes a few minutes to move

Salad Balance usually starts going up within about 15 minutes of a job starting. If a job only just kicked off, give it
a little time.

## A job ends before you expected

Customers start, stop and [move their jobs](/troubleshooting/not-earning/how-does-reallocation-work) all day, so a job
can wrap up sooner than you'd think. You're still credited for the time it ran, and your PC is available for the next
job right away without you doing anything.

## Task Manager shows no GPU activity

Task Manager often shows little or nothing while Salad is hard at work, because it's
[watching a different graph by default](/faq/jobs/why-isnt-task-manager-showing-any-activity-for-salad). The Performance
tab in the Salad app shows what your GPU is really doing.

## Salad stopped after a restart

A [Windows update](/faq/jobs/how-windows-updates-affect-salad) or any other restart stops the job you're running. Once
your PC is back up, check that Salad is Chopping again, and expect a bit of "finding a job" before the next one shows
up.

## Chopping on a laptop

Laptops are compatible with Salad, but they're more susceptible to heat issues than desktops. You may need to take extra
steps, like additional cooling, to keep yours stably Chopping. See [Heat and fan noise](/faq/jobs/heat-and-fan-noise).

## When to take a closer look

- A [Degraded notification](/troubleshooting/degraded-state) under the bell icon in the app.
- [Many hours of "finding a job"](/troubleshooting/not-earning/finding-job-for-hours) on a GPU the
  [Network Monitor](/faq/jobs/network-monitor) shows as in demand.
- A job running for hours with [nothing credited](/troubleshooting/not-earning/job-running-but-no-earnings) in your
  Earnings Summary.
