These are the GPUs that can run [GPU container jobs](/faq/jobs/how-do-i-get-container-jobs) on Salad, and what each one
earns per hour while a job is running. The rate depends on the job's priority, which the customer sets. See
[How Priority Levels Work](/faq/jobs/how-priority-levels-work).

Rates are as of November 1, 2026. Search for your card, or star it to add it to My GPUs and see only your own cards.

<GpuList />

The RTX 4080 Super, RTX 4070 Super and RTX 2080 Super are counted with the RTX 4080, RTX 4070 and RTX 2080.

## Understand Your Earnings

- The [electricity calculator](https://salad.com/download) estimates your profit after power costs.
- The [Network Monitor](https://salad.com/earn/demand) shows the demand for each GPU right now.
- The [earning rates page](https://salad.com/earning-rates/) shows what each GPU has actually been earning on Salad.
- [How Priority Levels Work](/faq/jobs/how-priority-levels-work) explains why the same GPU earns different rates.

## If Your GPU Isn't Listed

Cards not listed here can still earn, and potentially significantly, through
[CPU jobs](/faq/compatibility/should-i-enable-my-gpu-my-cpu-or-both),
[bandwidth sharing](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing) and opting in to
[background mining workloads](/faq/jobs/mining-as-a-backup-workload).

## Think Your GPU Should Be Supported?

If your GPU is unsupported currently and you believe it would qualify for
[GPU container jobs](/faq/jobs/how-do-i-get-container-jobs), please reach out to us directly at
[support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com). Please include the machine's other specs (CPU, RAM) and how many you
have. If possible, leave the machine online on Salad as well. It helps us determine whether support is viable.
