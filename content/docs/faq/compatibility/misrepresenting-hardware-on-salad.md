---
title: Misrepresenting Hardware on Salad
---

Spoofing or otherwise misrepresenting your hardware on Salad is a breach of our
[Terms of Service](https://salad.com/terms/chefs/). Any person found doing so will be permanently banned from using
Salad's services or otherwise punished.

## What Counts as Hardware Spoofing?

Hardware spoofing is any attempt to make Salad believe your machine has different hardware than it actually does. This
includes, but is not limited to:

- Changing the reported model of your GPU or CPU, such as making an older GPU appear as a newer, in-demand model
- Faking the amount of VRAM, RAM, storage, or other system specifications
- Editing drivers, registry entries, or system files to alter how your hardware is identified
- Using third-party tools, scripts, or virtual machines to disguise or fake your hardware

## Why Does It Matter?

Salad assigns workloads based on the hardware each machine reports. Our customers rely on that information being
accurate, and workloads sent to a machine that can't actually run them will fail. Misrepresenting your hardware harms
our customers, the reliability of the Salad network, and every other Chef who depends on it.

## What If My Hardware Is Being Detected Incorrectly?

If Salad isn't recognizing your hardware correctly, don't try to work around it. Instead:

1. Check that your hardware is supported by reviewing
   [Is My Machine Compatible With Salad?](/faq/compatibility/is-my-machine-compatible-with-salad)
2. Make sure your GPU drivers are up to date by [reinstalling them](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers).
3. If the problem continues, contact [Salad Support](/guides/your-pc/how-to-create-a-support-ticket) with details about
   your issue so we can help.
