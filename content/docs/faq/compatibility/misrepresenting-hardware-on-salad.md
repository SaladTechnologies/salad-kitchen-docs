Spoofing or otherwise misrepresenting your hardware on Salad breaks our
[Terms of Service](https://salad.com/terms/chefs/). Anyone found doing it is permanently banned.

## What counts as hardware spoofing

Any attempt to make Salad believe your machine has different hardware than it does, including:

- Changing the reported model of your GPU or CPU, such as making an older card appear as a newer, in-demand model.
- Faking the amount of VRAM, RAM, storage or other specifications.
- Editing drivers, registry entries or system files to alter how hardware is identified.
- Using third-party tools, scripts or virtual machines to disguise or fake hardware.

## Why it matters

Jobs are assigned based on the hardware each machine reports. Customers rely on that being accurate, and a job sent to a
machine that can't run it fails. That harms the customer, the network's reliability, and every other Chef.

## If your hardware is detected incorrectly

Don't work around it. Instead:

1. Check that your hardware is supported:
   [Is my machine compatible with Salad?](/faq/compatibility/is-my-machine-compatible-with-salad)
2. See [What GPU or CPU is Salad detecting?](/guides/your-pc/what-gpu-or-cpu-is-salad-detecting) and
   [reinstall your GPU drivers](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers).
3. If it is still wrong, contact [Salad Support](/guides/your-pc/how-to-create-a-support-ticket) with your machine ID
   and a screenshot of what the app shows.
