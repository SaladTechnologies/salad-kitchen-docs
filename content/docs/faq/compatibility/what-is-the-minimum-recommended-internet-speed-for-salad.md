---
title: What is the minimum recommended internet speed for Salad?
description: The internet speeds you need for container jobs and for bandwidth sharing, and how to test yours.
---

## Container jobs

- **Download:** at least 20 Mbps required; 100 Mbps or more recommended. Faster connections qualify for more jobs, and a
  slow one can lead a customer to reallocate the job to a faster machine.
- **Upload:** 20 Mbps or more recommended.

Salad runs its own speed test when it starts, and the result may be lower than what a browser speed test reports. That
lower number is what the app uses to verify compatibility, not self-run tests. Recorded speeds are currently only used
to validate compatibility - even if speeds show up low, being above the minimum is sufficient to receive workloads. If
it falls short, see [How to improve internet speed for container jobs](/guides/your-pc/improve-internet-speed).

## Bandwidth sharing

Requirements vary by region. For most regions:

- **Download:** at least 20 Mbps required; 50 Mbps or more recommended for consistent earnings.
- **Upload:** at least as fast as your download.

See [What is bandwidth sharing?](/faq/jobs/what-is-bandwidth-sharing) and
[Bandwidth sharing troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/not-earning/bandwidth-sharing-troubleshooting).

## Testing your connection

Any browser speed test works, such as [Speedtest by Ookla](https://www.speedtest.net/) or [Fast.com](https://fast.com/).
Test over the connection your PC actually uses (ideally a wired one), and remember that Salad's own measurement may read
lower. If you are below the minimums, a wired connection and a quieter network help more than most people expect;
otherwise it is a conversation with your ISP.
