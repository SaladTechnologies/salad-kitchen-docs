---
title: GDPR and privacy requests
description: How to ask Salad for a copy of your personal data or for its erasure, and what to expect in return.
---

You can ask Salad for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to erase it. Email
[support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com) from the email address your Salad account is registered to and say which
request you are making. We confirm receipt and answer within one month.

<Callout type="info">
  Requests must come from the account's own email address so we can verify who is asking. We cannot act on a request
  sent from another address.
</Callout>

## Requesting a copy of your data

Ask for "a copy of my personal data" (a subject access request). We send you a machine-readable file containing:

- Your account details.
- The machines registered to your account.
- Your earnings and Salad Balance history.
- Your redemptions.
- Your conversations with Salad Support.
- Your mailing-list records.

## Requesting erasure

Ask for "erasure of my personal data". We delete the account and the personal data linked to it, including at the
service providers we use. Erasure is irreversible and any remaining Salad Balance is lost, so redeem it first. The
practical steps are the same as [deleting your account](/faq/your-account/how-can-i-delete-my-account).
