---
title: Reporting a security issue
description: How to report a vulnerability in Salad to the team, and what to expect after you do.
---

If you have found a security issue in the Salad app, salad.com or any other Salad service, email the details to
[support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com). Reports are forwarded to the engineering team and reviewed.

<Callout type="info" title="No bug bounty">
  Salad does not run a bug bounty programme and does not pay for vulnerability reports. Findings are welcome and are
  looked at, but there is no reward for them.
</Callout>

## What to include

- What the issue is and what it allows an attacker to do.
- Steps to reproduce it, with the affected URL, app version or component.
- Any screenshots or logs that show the problem.

## Ground rules

- Do not test against production accounts, Salad Balance or other Chefs' data. Use your own account and your own
  machines.
- Do not run denial-of-service tests, spam or social engineering against Salad staff or Chefs.
- Do not publish the issue before Salad has had a reasonable chance to fix it.

If you are not reporting a vulnerability but think your own account has been compromised, see
[Keeping your account secure](/faq/your-account/keeping-your-account-secure) and contact [Salad Support](/contact).
