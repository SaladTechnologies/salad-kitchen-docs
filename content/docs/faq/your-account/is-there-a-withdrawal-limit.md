---
title: Is there a withdrawal limit?
description: There is a daily cap on how much Salad Balance one person can redeem, reset at midnight UTC.
---

There is a maximum daily withdrawal limit of $1000 of Salad Balance per person. This resets each day at midnight UTC.
Unused allowance does not carry over: if you spend $200 on day 1, your cap on day 2 is still $1000, not $1800.

The limit is applied to the cost of the reward on the Storefront, not the value of the reward itself. It applies to all
redemptions and purchases from Salad, including charity donations.
