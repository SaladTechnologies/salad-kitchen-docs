---
title: Can I convert my earnings into cryptocurrency?
description:
  Yes - you can redeem RENDER tokens from the Storefront to a Solana wallet. No other direct crypto conversion is
  supported.
---

Yes. You can redeem [RENDER tokens](https://salad.com/store/search?q=RENDER&size=n_20_n), the native token of the Render
Network, from the Storefront and have them sent to your Solana wallet.

Before your first redemption you need a Solana wallet holding a small amount of SOL to create the RENDER token account,
and turning RENDER into regular money usually means an exchange account with identity verification. The
[How to redeem RENDER on Salad](/rewards/redeeming-your-rewards/how-to-redeem-render-on-salad) guide walks through every
step.

## Other rewards

RENDER is the only cryptocurrency reward Salad supports directly. Other items in the
[Storefront](https://salad.com/store) may be convertible to cryptocurrency on your own, but that is not an official
method and is not guaranteed to work. Your Salad account is not a wallet - see
[Can I deposit or withdraw cryptocurrency?](/faq/your-account/can-i-deposit-or-withdraw-cryptocurrency-to-my-salad-account).
