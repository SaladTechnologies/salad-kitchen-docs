---
title: Keeping your account secure
description: A short checklist to protect your Salad account and the Salad Balance in it.
---

Your Salad account holds Salad Balance that can be redeemed instantly, which makes it a target. Treat it like a small
bank account. These steps cover almost every account takeover we see:

- **Use a unique, long password for your email account.** Whoever controls your email can log in to Salad. A password
  manager makes unique passwords painless.
- **Add a passkey to your Salad account.** A passkey replaces one-time login codes and cannot be phished. See
  [Salad app passkeys](/guides/using-salad/salad-app-passkeys).
- **Store your passkey backup codes off the PC.** Keep them in a password manager, on paper or on a USB key - not only
  on the machine that runs Salad. If you lose both the passkey and the codes, support cannot remove the passkey and the
  account has to be [deleted](/faq/your-account/how-can-i-delete-my-account).
- **Never share a login code.** Salad will never ask for your one-time code, not even Salad Support. Only enter it on
  salad.com or in the Salad app. Delete codes you did not request - see
  [I've received a suspicious login attempt](/faq/your-account/ive-received-a-suspicious-login-attempt).
- **Download Salad only from [salad.com](https://salad.com).** Installers from anywhere else may be modified.
- **Do not give other people access to your email account** or leave it logged in on shared PCs.

If you think someone already has access to your account, contact [Salad Support](/contact) straight away.
