---
title: How can I delete my account?
description: How to request deletion of your Salad account by email, and what you lose when it is deleted.
---

To delete your Salad account, email [support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com) from the email address the account is
registered to. Support cannot delete an account on a request sent from a different address.

<Callout type="warn" title="Deletion is irreversible">
  Any Salad Balance left in the account is lost when it is deleted and cannot be restored. Redeem what you want to keep
  first.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Spend your remaining Salad Balance

Redeem anything you want from the [Storefront](https://salad.com/store) before you ask for deletion. Balance in a
deleted account is gone for good.

</Step>
<Step>

### Email support from the account's address

Send an email to [support@salad.com](mailto:support@salad.com) with:

- Your Salad username.
- The email address of the account (the one you are sending from).
- A short note that you want the account deleted.

</Step>
<Step>

### Wait for confirmation

Support flags the account for deletion and it is usually removed within minutes. You get a reply confirming it is done,
and your email address is removed from Salad's mailing lists.

</Step>
</Steps>

## After deletion

- You can create a new Salad account with the same email address later. Nothing from the old account carries over.
- If you lost your passkey and your backup codes, deletion is the only way to get going again: support cannot remove a
  passkey from an account. Delete the account and start a new one.
- If you only want a copy of your data, or want your data erased under GDPR, see
  [GDPR and privacy requests](/faq/your-account/gdpr-and-privacy-requests).
