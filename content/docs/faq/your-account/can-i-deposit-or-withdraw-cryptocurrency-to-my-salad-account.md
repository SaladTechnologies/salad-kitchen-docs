---
title: Can I deposit or withdraw cryptocurrency to my Salad account?
description: No - your Salad account is not a cryptocurrency wallet. Balance is spent in the Storefront, not withdrawn.
---

No. Your Salad account is not a cryptocurrency wallet and cannot be used like one. You cannot deposit cryptocurrency
into it, and you cannot withdraw your Salad Balance as cryptocurrency or as cash to a bank account.

Salad Balance is spent on rewards in the [Storefront](https://salad.com/store). Some of those rewards are
cryptocurrency, such as [RENDER tokens](https://salad.com/store/search?q=RENDER&size=n_20_n) - see
[Can I convert my earnings into cryptocurrency?](/faq/your-account/can-i-convert-my-earnings-into-cryptocurrency).

## I found a wallet address in my logs. What is it?

Wallet addresses in your Salad logs belong to Salad's mining payouts and are shared across many Chefs; they are not
yours. Your machine ID is what ties the balance you earn to your account. Anything you send to one of those addresses
will **not** appear in your Salad Balance and is lost permanently.
