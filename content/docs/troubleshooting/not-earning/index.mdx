---
title: Not Earning
description:
  Start here when Salad shows Finding Job for a long time, earnings dropped, a job runs but pays nothing, or jobs keep
  restarting.
---

Most "not earning" reports fall into one of a few patterns, and each has its own page. If the app shows a **Degraded**
notification, go to [Degraded State](/troubleshooting/degraded-state) first - that's what is blocking jobs.

<Callout type="info" title="Check your earnings history before anything else">
  The app's window can lag behind what the machine is doing. Open your [Earnings
  Summary](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary): if balance is being credited every hour, the machine is working and
  the display just hasn't caught up.
</Callout>
