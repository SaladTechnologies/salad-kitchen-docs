---
title: Salad shows "Finding Job" for hours
description:
  What "Finding Job" means, how long it can normally last, how to tell low demand from a problem on your PC, and what to
  check before contacting support.
---

**What it means.** "Finding Job" is the app waiting for the network to assign a container job and downloading it when
one arrives. It appears between every two jobs, and jobs are often short: customers start and stop workloads through the
day, so a machine can cycle through many jobs with short waits in between. That's normal and you're paid for every
minute a job runs.

**How long is normal?** A few minutes between jobs is typical. An hour or two happens when demand for your GPU dips.
Many hours with nothing - on a GPU the [Network Monitor](https://salad.com/earn/demand) shows as in demand - is worth
checking.

## First, check whether it's really idle

Open your [Earnings Summary](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary). If balance was credited in the last hour, the
machine is running a job and the app's display is behind. Close the window with the X and reopen it from the tray icon
(don't use **Exit** - that stops the running job).

## What to check, in order

<Steps>
<Step>

### Degraded notifications

Open the bell icon. Any Degraded notification means a check is failing and no container jobs are offered until it's
fixed. Follow the guide for it under [Degraded State](/troubleshooting/degraded-state).

</Step>
<Step>

### Demand for your GPU

On the [Network Monitor](https://salad.com/earn/demand), find your exact card. Low or moderate demand means longer
waits; the machine is fine. Set up a [demand alert](/guides/getting-jobs/how-to-set-up-a-demand-alert) and leave Salad
running - restarting it does not move you up the queue and
[can hurt your chances](/guides/getting-jobs/restarting-salad-for-new-jobs).

</Step>
<Step>

### The first job after a fresh install

A new install (or reinstall) must complete a short compatibility check - a download of roughly 60 MB that has to finish
once - before paid jobs are offered. On a slow or filtered connection that alone can take a long time; see
[Downloads slow or failing](/troubleshooting/container-jobs/downloads-slow-or-failing).

</Step>
<Step>

### The basics

Both GPU and CPU enabled under **Settings > Configure Hardware**; at least 70 GB free on C:; virtualization on; no VPN
or proxy; nothing else using the GPU; the PC not asleep (see
[Disable sleep mode](/guides/using-salad/how-to-disable-sleep-mode)). The full list is on
[Not getting container jobs](/troubleshooting/not-earning/not-getting-container-jobs).

</Step>
<Step>

### Your connection

Salad runs a speed test at startup and the network uses it when assigning jobs. If the test ran while your connection
was still coming back after an outage or a router reboot, the reading sticks until Salad restarts. Once the line is back
to normal, Exit Salad from the tray icon and start it again so the test reruns.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Please don't reinstall to get jobs">
  Reinstalling, unregistering the WSL environment or clearing Salad's folders resets the compatibility check and the
  image cache, so the next jobs start from zero downloads. It doesn't change what the network offers you.
</Callout>

## Still nothing after a day?

Send [Salad Support](/contact) your machine ID, the time the wait started with your time zone, and your logs folder
zipped ([how to find it](/guides/using-salad/how-to-find-your-salad-log-files)). We can see whether the network offered
your machine jobs that didn't start, or simply had none for your hardware.
