---
title: 'Job running but no earnings'
description:
  "What to check when Salad shows a job running (GPU busy) but your balance isn't increasing: miners that pay cents, a
  stale display, or a job that isn't actually running."
---

**Most of the time this is one of three things:**

1. **The running job is the crypto miner**, which pays very little on modern cards - a few cents a day - so the balance
   barely moves even though the GPU is at 100%.
2. **The app's display hasn't refreshed**: the job finished or changed and the window still shows the old state.
3. **The job isn't running at all**: it's stuck downloading or repeatedly restarting, and the activity indicator is
   misleading.

## Tell them apart

<Steps>
<Step>

### Look at the Earnings Summary, not the Earn page

Open your [Earnings Summary](/guides/using-salad/earnings-summary) and look at the last few hours. Small, regular
credits mean the machine is paid for what it's doing (probably mining). No credits at all for several hours means the
job isn't really running - go to step 3.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check which workload is running

On the **Earn** page the job name shows whether it's a container job or a miner. Miners run only when no container job
is available for your hardware. If you'd rather the card waited for container jobs instead of mining for cents, turn
mining off under **Settings > Workload Types** ([details](/faq/jobs/mining-as-a-backup-workload)). Turning it off
doesn't bring container jobs sooner; it just stops the low-paying fallback.

</Step>
<Step>

### Refresh the display

Close the Salad window with the X and reopen it from the tray icon. Don't choose **Exit** - that stops the running job.
If the display now matches the Earnings Summary, nothing was wrong.

</Step>
<Step>

### If nothing has been credited for hours

Check the bell icon for a Degraded notification ([Degraded State](/troubleshooting/degraded-state)), then look at the
activity indicator: stuck on _Downloading_ means a download problem
([Downloads slow or failing](/troubleshooting/container-jobs/downloads-slow-or-failing)); flipping between
_Initializing_ and _Running_ every few minutes means the job keeps restarting
([Jobs keep failing or restarting](/troubleshooting/not-earning/why-do-jobs-keep-failing-or-restarting)).

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Earnings are credited while the job runs">
  Container jobs pay as they go, not at the end. If a job ran for hours and you were paid nothing, the machine wasn't
  actually running it - the logs will show why.
</Callout>

## Still stuck?

Send [Salad Support](/contact) your machine ID, the job name shown on the Earn page, the hours you expected earnings for
(with time zone), and your logs folder zipped ([how to find it](/guides/using-salad/how-to-find-your-salad-log-files)).
We can see what the machine ran and what it was paid for, hour by hour.
