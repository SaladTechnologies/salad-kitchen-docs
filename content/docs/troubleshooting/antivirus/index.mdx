title: Antivirus
description:
  Why antivirus and firewall products flag Salad, which folders to whitelist, and step-by-step exclusion guides for each
  major product.
---

Antivirus products regularly stop Salad from running. The programs Salad downloads to earn you balance are well-known
open-source miners (XMRig, Rigel, T-Rex and others), and almost every antivirus flags miners on sight, usually as a
"Trojan", "PUA" or "vulnerable driver" detection, even though the files are unmodified releases. Salad's container
environment is also a virtual machine, which some security suites treat as suspicious. Neither detection means your PC
is infected; it means the antivirus does not know what Salad is.

## What to whitelist

Add these two folders to your antivirus exclusions:

- `C:\Program Files\Salad` (the Salad app)
- `C:\ProgramData\Salad` (the container environment and the miners, under `workloads`)

If your product only accepts single files, add every `.exe` under `C:\ProgramData\Salad\workloads`. If you would rather
not whitelist miners at all, turn mining off in the Salad app's [Settings](/guides/using-salad/salad-app-settings) and
Salad runs container jobs only.
