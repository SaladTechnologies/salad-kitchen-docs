---
title: 'Insufficient VRAM and high VRAM activity'
description:
  'Fix Degraded State errors 13, 24, 25 ("Insufficient VRAM") and 18 ("High VRAM activity"): too much of your GPU''s
  memory is in use when the compatibility check runs.'
---

**What it means.** A customer's job needs the whole of your graphics card's memory (VRAM). Before offering you a job,
Salad measures how much of it is already in use. **13, 24 and 25** mean too much was taken; **18** means something was
reading and writing VRAM heavily at the time. The check isn't about how much VRAM the card has - a 12 GB card passes
when it's free - it's about what else is holding it.

**Why it happens.** Something is using the card while Salad checks it:

- A game, a local AI model (Ollama, LM Studio, Stable Diffusion), a video editor, OBS, a browser with hardware
  acceleration and many tabs, or wallpaper engines.
- Windows itself on high-resolution or multiple monitors, plus RGB and monitoring overlays.
- A second dedicated GPU: the check may land on the busy card.
- ECC memory enabled on workstation cards, which reserves part of the VRAM (error 101 - see
  [How to disable ECC](/guides/your-pc/how-to-disable-ecc)).

## What to do

<Steps>
<Step>

### See what's using the GPU

Open Task Manager (**Ctrl + Shift + Esc**), go to **Performance > GPU** and look at _Dedicated GPU memory usage_. On the
**Processes** tab, right-click a column header, enable **GPU** and **GPU engine**, and sort by GPU. Close anything above
a few percent, or anything holding more than a small amount of dedicated memory.

</Step>
<Step>

### Close background GPU users

Local AI tools and overlays keep models and buffers in VRAM even when idle. Quit them fully (tray icon > Exit), not just
the window. Salad is designed to run while you're away from the PC with everything else closed.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check for a second GPU

With two dedicated cards, disable the one you don't want Salad to use under **Settings > Configure Hardware**
([why](/faq/compatibility/can-i-use-multiple-gpus)).

</Step>
<Step>

### Retry

Click **Retry** on the notification, or restart Salad. The check runs again before the next job.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Nothing running, still failing?">
  A driver that reports memory wrongly can also trigger this. If Task Manager shows the card nearly empty and the error
  persists, do a clean reinstall of the GPU driver: [How to reinstall your GPU
  drivers](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers).
</Callout>
