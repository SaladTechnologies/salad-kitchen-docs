  What the Degraded State notification in the Salad app means, how to read the error code, and which guide fixes each

"Degraded" means your machine is online and Chopping, but a check has failed, so it is not being offered container jobs
(the best-paying work). Nothing is broken on Salad's side: the app found something on your PC that would stop a
customer's job from running, and it tells you what, so you can fix it and get back to full earnings.

## How to read the notification

Open the notification from the bell icon in the Salad app or from the Earn page. It has a title (for example "GPU Power
Limit Set"), a short explanation and, in most cases, an error number at the end ("Error 14"). The title tells you which
guide below to follow; the number is what support will ask for if you get stuck.

After you fix the cause, click **Retry** in the notification or restart Salad. The check runs again before every
container job, so the Degraded badge clears on its own once it passes.

<Callout type="info" title="Reinstalling rarely fixes this">
  Most Degraded notifications are caused by something outside the Salad app: a driver, a Windows setting, another
  program using the GPU, or a laptop power mode. Reinstalling Salad doesn't change any of those. Follow the guide for
  your notification first.
</Callout>

## Still degraded after following the guide?

Send [Salad Support](/contact) three things: your machine ID (the first 8 characters are enough), a screenshot of the
notification showing the error number, and your logs folder zipped
([how to find it](/guides/using-salad/how-to-find-your-salad-log-files)). Send the logs soon after the problem - they
rotate after a few days.
