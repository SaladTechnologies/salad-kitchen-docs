---
title: GPU performance low
description:
  Fix Degraded State error 42 ("GPU performance is too low compared to similar GPUs") and error 23 ("GPU failed health
  test").
---

**What it means.** As part of the compatibility check, Salad runs a short benchmark on your card and compares the result
with what the same GPU model normally achieves on the network. **42** means your card scored below that; **23** means
the health test didn't complete. Until a run passes, the machine isn't offered container jobs.

**Why it happens.** The benchmark measures the whole machine's ability to feed the GPU, so the usual causes are:

- Another program using the card during the test (see [High GPU usage](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/high-gpu-usage)).
- A **power limit** or undervolt: the card can't reach its normal clocks. On laptops this comes from the vendor's power
  mode, not from Windows. See [GPU power limit](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/gpu-power-limit).
- **Heat**: the card throttles part-way through the test.
- A damaged driver, or a driver reporting the card wrongly - common after an in-place driver update.
- The card driving several high-resolution displays while being tested.
- Hardware problems: a card in a slow PCIe slot, or a faulty card (error 23 in particular).

## What to do

<Steps>
<Step>

### Make sure nothing else is using the GPU

Task Manager > Processes > sort by **GPU**. Close games, local AI tools, overlays and capture software. Fully exit tray
programs.

</Step>
<Step>

### Remove power limits and undervolts

Set MSI Afterburner (or your vendor's tool) back to defaults: power limit 100%, core and memory offsets 0. On a laptop:
vendor performance mode (ASUS Armoury Crate _Turbo_, MSI Center _Extreme Performance_, Lenovo Vantage _Performance_),
the original charger, and Windows power mode _Best performance_. Full guide:
[GPU power limit](/troubleshooting/degraded-state/gpu-power-limit).

</Step>
<Step>

### Check temperatures and airflow

If the card is hot before the test starts, it won't score well. Clear dust, check fans, and on laptops use a cooling
pad. Let the card cool, then Retry.

</Step>
<Step>

### Clean-reinstall the GPU driver

With nothing running and no power limit, a low score usually means the driver: follow
[How to reinstall your GPU drivers](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers) and reboot.

</Step>
<Step>

### Retry

Click **Retry** on the notification or restart Salad. Each container job triggers a fresh check, so one good run is all
it takes.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Laptops and the display GPU">
  On laptops where the screen is wired straight to the NVIDIA card (ASUS "Ultimate", MSI "Discrete graphics" modes), the
  card is never fully idle. If you keep failing with everything closed, try the hybrid/standard mode as a test so the
  card is left entirely to Salad.
</Callout>
