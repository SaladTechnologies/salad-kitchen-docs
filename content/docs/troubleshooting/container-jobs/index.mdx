  'Problems that stop a container job from being downloaded or set up on your PC: slow downloads, storage errors and

---

Container jobs are downloaded into Salad's Linux container environment and then started on your GPU. The pages here
cover what goes wrong _before_ a job runs. If the app shows a Degraded notification, start with
[Degraded State](/troubleshooting/degraded-state); if jobs download fine but you're not earning, see
[Not Earning](/troubleshooting/not-earning).
