---
title: General troubleshooting tips
description:
  The general checks that fix most unexplained Salad app problems, in order, and where to go when the app gives you a
  specific notification.
---

If the app shows a notification, follow its guide first - [Degraded State](/troubleshooting/degraded-state) lists every
one. These general steps are for when the app misbehaves without saying why. Try Salad again after each.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Restart the app and the PC

Exit Salad from the tray icon (right-click > Exit), then restart Windows. A surprising amount is fixed here.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm the PC meets the requirements

[Is my machine compatible with Salad?](/faq/compatibility/is-my-machine-compatible-with-salad) - a dedicated GPU on the
Network Monitor, enough RAM and free space on C:, virtualization enabled, no VPN.

</Step>
<Step>

### Whitelist Salad in your antivirus and firewall

[Antivirus guides](/troubleshooting/antivirus). This covers the app, the container environment and the backup miners.

</Step>
<Step>

### Update WSL and the container environment

In PowerShell as Administrator run `wsl --update`, then `wsl --shutdown`, then start Salad. If WSL itself is missing or
broken: [What is WSL?](/guides/your-pc/what-is-wsl).

</Step>
<Step>

### Close everything else

Salad needs the GPU to itself. Close games, local AI tools, overlays and capture software, and leave the PC idle.
[Close games and other programs](/guides/getting-jobs/close-games-and-other-programs).

</Step>
<Step>

### Update, or clean-reinstall, your GPU driver

Many "random" failures are a damaged driver:
[How to reinstall your GPU drivers](/guides/your-pc/how-to-reinstall-gpu-drivers). If Salad stopped working right after
a driver update, a clean install of the previous version is worth a try.

</Step>
<Step>

### Update Windows and the Visual C++ runtime

Install pending Windows updates
([Microsoft's guide](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/update-windows-3c5ae7fc-9fb6-9af1-1984-b5e0412c556a))
and the [latest Visual C++ Redistributable](https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe), then reboot.

</Step>
<Step>

### Last resort: reinstall Salad

[Uninstall Salad](/guides/using-salad/how-to-uninstall-salad), reboot, install from
[salad.com/download](https://salad.com/download). Reinstalling doesn't touch drivers, WSL or Windows settings, which is
why it comes last.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Still stuck?

Contact [Salad Support](/contact) with a description of what happens, your machine ID, and your logs folder zipped
([how to find it](/guides/using-salad/how-to-find-your-salad-log-files)).
[How to create a support ticket](/guides/your-pc/how-to-create-a-support-ticket) lists everything that speeds up an
answer.
