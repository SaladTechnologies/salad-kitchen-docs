---
title: "I can't get Salad to download"
description:
  'What to do when the Salad installer will not download: browser download blocks, antivirus, cache and trying another
  browser.'
---

The Salad installer is a Windows `.exe`, and browsers and antivirus tools are cautious about those. Work through these
in order.

1. **Allow the download in your browser.** Chrome and Edge often hold `.exe` downloads for review. Open the downloads
   list (the arrow icon at the top right, or **Ctrl + J**) and choose **Keep** or **Download anyway**. In Edge, click
   the **...** next to the blocked file and choose **Keep**.
2. **Check your antivirus.** Some antivirus products quarantine the installer as soon as it lands. Look in the product's
   quarantine or protection log, restore the file and add it to the exclusions. See
   [Antivirus](/troubleshooting/antivirus); the installer is genuine, and you can
   [verify its hash](/guides/your-pc/how-to-check-if-your-salad-installer-is-safe).
3. **Try another browser** or a private window. Extensions and a corrupt cache can break downloads; clearing the browser
   cache also helps.
4. **Download from GitHub instead.** The same installer is published on the
   [Salad GitHub releases page](https://github.com/SaladTechnologies/Salad-Applications/releases).
5. **Check your network.** School, work and some ISP networks block executable downloads. Try a different network or a
   mobile hotspot.

If none of these work, tell [Salad Support](/contact) which browser you use and what message the browser or antivirus
shows.
